# BeastLink SEO research

Research date: 2026-08-11

## Confirmed identity and facts

- **Developer / publisher:** Grove Street Games.
- **Current release state:** Steam lists Early Access for August 18, 2026; the official site says Early Access August 2026. Steam still presents the product as not yet released on the research date.
- **Game identity:** a giant multiplayer Kaiju game where humans, vehicles, and Beasts collide in destructible urban environments.
- **Confirmed systems:** serum-based BeastLinking, human/vehicle/Beast roles, SuperDestruction, online PvP/PvE framing, and maps built for up to 32 players.
- **Early Access snapshot:** Steam’s current developer FAQ states 3 maps, 4 Beasts, multiplayer modes, dedicated online servers, progression, offline modes, a tutorial, and initial single-player missions. These are time-sensitive Early Access claims.
- **PC requirements:** Steam lists Windows 10, 16 GB RAM minimum / 32 GB recommended, DirectX 12, RTX 2060 or RX 5600 XT minimum-class graphics, 30 GB storage, and broadband internet.

## Page opportunities selected

The existing four-Guide experiment is retained:

- `/beastlink/` — game identity, current Early Access timing, platforms, and core loop.
- `/beastlink/release-date/` — Early Access date, Early Access vs 1.0, and platform caveats.
- `/beastlink/gameplay/` — human/vehicle/Beast roles, destruction, multiplayer, and current Early Access snapshot.
- `/beastlink/system-requirements/` — Steam PC minimum and recommended requirements.
- `/` — target-specific entrance to the Hub and Guides.

## Intentionally omitted

No separate named-Beast roster, map guide, ability tier list, 1.0 roadmap page, console store guide, or competitive strategy guide is added. Early Access content is expected to change, and a small set of pages is more defensible than turning provisional roadmap material into permanent claims.

## Migration decision

The target-specific static Hub + Guide architecture and all public URLs are preserved. The only public structural cleanup is replacing the generic LaunchWire chrome with BeastLink-specific branding. No deployment or Vercel configuration change is made.
