## What is BeastLink gameplay?

BeastLink is described by its official site and Steam listing as a giant multiplayer Kaiju game. Humans, vehicles, and Beasts collide in destructible urban environments. The confirmed system framing includes serum-based BeastLinking, SuperDestruction, and online PvP/PvE modes.

The official materials also frame maps for up to **32 players**. That is a player-capacity statement from the reviewed sources, not a claim that every mode or map has identical rules.

## Current Early Access snapshot

Steam's developer FAQ, checked on 2026-08-11, lists the following current snapshot:

- 3 maps and 4 Beasts
- multiplayer modes and dedicated online servers
- progression and offline modes
- a tutorial and initial single-player missions

These details are explicitly time-sensitive Early Access information. They may change as development continues and should not be read as a final 1.0 content promise.

## What this Guide does not claim

This page does not create a named-Beast roster, ability tier list, complete map guide, or competitive strategy guide. The available sources do not support those deeper pages with enough stable detail.
