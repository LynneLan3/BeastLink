What is BeastLink?

BeastLink is Grove Street Games’ first new original IP in over a decade. Official copy describes a world where humanity survives in shattered strongholds while colossal creatures rule the ruins. Players gather serum, establish a BeastLink with dormant Kaiju, and shift from tactical human / vehicle play into city-scale Beast combat.

The studio’s proprietary SuperDestruction system is the technical centerpiece: fully networked, physics-based destruction where maps can be reshaped during play. Steam and the official site both frame the fantasy as “build your Link and unleash your power before it consumes you.”

BeastLink Release Date

Steam currently lists a planned Early Access date of August 18, 2026. The official website states Early Access August 2026. Grove Street’s official press release says Early Access launches on console and PC “this summer.”

For 1.0, the Steam Early Access FAQ currently points to a content roadmap around Summer 2027, with the note that the full-game timeline may shift based on community discoveries.

  • PC (Steam) — Early Access planned for August 18, 2026; wishlist / store page live

Nintendo Switch / Switch 2 versions are not yet officially confirmed. Exact same-day console unlock timing beyond the Summer / August 2026 Early Access framing should be verified on each console storefront closer to launch.

Gameplay Overview

Official Steam and site features emphasize:

  • Become the Beast — earn serum, Link with dormant Kaiju, unlock unique abilities
  • Human, vehicle, and Beast roles on one battlefield
  • SuperDestruction — networked, physics-based city destruction
  • Multiplayer mayhem — maps designed for up to 32 players and multiple active Beasts
  • Unique Beasts — growing roster with distinct combat styles
  • Story framing — uncover the outbreak and the cost of keeping humanity alive

Steam’s Early Access notes currently describe launch EA content with 3 maps, 4 beasts, multiplayer modes (Convergence and Battle Arena), weapons and vehicles, character / Kaiju progression, dedicated online servers, offline modes, a tutorial, and the first few single-player missions.

BeastLink FAQ

When does BeastLink come out?

Steam lists Early Access for August 18, 2026. The official site says Early Access August 2026.

Is BeastLink multiplayer?

Yes. Official materials describe large-scale multiplayer with maps built for up to 32 players, plus PvP or PvE combat framing on Steam. Early Access also lists offline modes and early story missions.

Is BeastLink on PS5 and Xbox?

Grove Street’s official announcement lists PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and Steam PC for Early Access. Confirm console storefront pages nearer launch for exact unlock details.

Who developed BeastLink?

Grove Street Games develops and publishes BeastLink.
