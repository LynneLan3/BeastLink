BeastLink Gameplay

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

This page summarizes officially confirmed gameplay systems for BeastLink. It is a pre-release / Early Access reference based on the Steam store page, the official website, and Grove Street Games’ announcement — not post-launch playtesting.

Core loop: human, vehicle, Beast

Official materials describe three layers of asymmetrical play in the same urban battlefield:

  • On foot — navigate collapsing cities and fight as a human
  • Vehicles — cars, helicopters, tanks, and aircraft are cited in Steam copy
  • Beast / Kaiju — Link with dormant Beasts and fight at city-destroying scale

Players gather the coveted serum, then use it to establish a BeastLink. The stronger the Link grows, the more power you command — and the more it threatens to consume you, according to official framing.

SuperDestruction

Grove Street’s proprietary SuperDestruction system is the standout technical feature. Official copy describes a fully networked, physics-based destruction system where buildings, city blocks, and the skyline can be reshaped during matches. Press materials cite over 250,000 destructible objects and tens of millions of individual pieces.

Steam feature copy emphasizes collapsing buildings, carving new paths, weaponizing the environment, and maps where “nothing is off limits.”

Beasts and progression

Beasts have unique abilities and combat styles. Official press named four launch creatures:

  • Horned Lizard
  • Bull Shark
  • Vampire Bat
  • Mandrill

Steam’s Early Access FAQ currently states the EA version contains 4 beasts, with a 1.0 plan that includes 8 beasts in total. Detailed ability trees, balance numbers, and full unlock order beyond that public framing are not fully published as a complete public list.

Steam also states players can progress characters and Kaiju on dedicated online servers during Early Access.

Multiplayer and modes

BeastLink is positioned as a large-scale multiplayer experience. Official site / Steam feature copy describes huge maps built for up to 32 players and multiple active Beasts. Steam’s short description also frames intense PvP or PvE combat.

Steam Early Access notes currently list multiplayer modes Convergence and Battle Arena, alongside offline modes, a tutorial, and the first few single-player missions.

Exact match rules, ranked systems, party sizes, and post-EA mode roadmaps beyond those named modes are not fully detailed in the store materials used for this page.

Early Access content snapshot

According to Steam’s current Early Access FAQ, the EA version includes:

  • 3 maps
  • 4 beasts
  • Multiplayer modes: Convergence & Battle Arena
  • Dozens of weapons and vehicles
  • Dedicated online servers with character / Kaiju progression
  • Offline modes, a tutorial, and early story missions

The developers say EA will receive frequent updates and larger content drops as new maps, beasts, or scenario types are ready.

What this page does not cover yet

  • Full Beast ability trees or balance patches
  • Complete vehicle / weapon catalogs
  • Map walkthroughs or meta guides
  • Post-launch competitive rankings
