BeastLink Release Date

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

BeastLink Early Access is currently planned for August 18, 2026 on Steam.

Confirmed Early Access date (Steam)

Steam planned Early Access date: August 18, 2026

This date appears on the official Steam store page for BeastLink (app ID 2962780). The official website (playbeastlink.com) currently states Early Access August 2026.

Grove Street Games’ May 6, 2026 announcement said BeastLink would launch into Early Access on console and PC “this summer,” with closed beta weekends starting May 8, 2026.

Early Access vs. 1.0

August 2026 is framed as Early Access, not the finished 1.0 launch. On Steam, Grove Street currently says its Early Access content roadmap puts 1.0 around Summer 2027, while noting the full-game timeline may shift if community discoveries lead to additional content.

Planned 1.0 targets listed in the Steam Early Access FAQ include more content such as 12 maps based on three real-world cities, 8 beasts, 10 full story missions with online co-op, and additional game modes — described as a starting point for the broader roadmap.
